# Method

## 1. Pre-class

5–10 minutes before the voice session. Listen to the primary British Council audio at least twice without reading the transcript. The goal is to activate the topic and get a first estimate of comprehension, not to complete the test perfectly.

## 2. Voice session

1. Warm-up
2. First controlled listening
3. Comprehension questions
4. Vocabulary / source exercise
5. Speaking transfer
6. Everyday role-play
7. Correction → repeat → transform
8. Short exit check
9. Mandatory session report

## 3. Post-class

The report records evidence, errors, useful phrases, pronunciation notes, Can-Do progress, homework, and the next session.

## External listening

A short external YouTube/audio item may be added when it directly reinforces the lesson topic and level. It is optional and must never displace the British Council primary source.
