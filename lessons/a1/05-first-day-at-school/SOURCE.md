# Source map — A1 Test 5: First day at school

**Level:** A1
**Source:** https://learnenglishteens.britishcouncil.org/skills/listening/a1-listening/first-day-school
**Audio:** https://learnenglishteens.britishcouncil.org/sites/teens/files/2024-08/A1_first_day_at_school.mp3

## Source exercise structure
- Preparation: sort the supplied words into **School** or **Home** categories.
- Exercise 1: 3 True/False statements about two students, their class, teacher and origin.
- Exercise 2: 4 gap-fill items taken from the conversation.

## Speaking transfer
Practise meeting a new person: name, class/work context, teacher/manager, where someone is from, and simple friendly responses.
