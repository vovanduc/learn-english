# PRE-CLASS — A1-05

**Time:** 5–10 minutes
**Topic:** Meeting someone at school / first day
**Primary audio:** https://learnenglishteens.britishcouncil.org/sites/teens/files/2024-08/A1_first_day_at_school.mp3

1. Think of 5 words you associate with school or work.
2. Listen once without pausing. Identify who meets whom and why.
3. Listen again and note 3–5 words or phrases you recognise.
4. Say aloud:
   - My name is ...
   - Nice to meet you.
   - I am from ...
   - I work / study ...

Do not read the transcript before the Voice session.
