# PRE-CLASS — A1-04

**Time:** 5–10 minutes
**Topic:** Describing people
**Primary audio:** https://learnenglishteens.britishcouncil.org/sites/teens/files/2024-08/A1_describing_people.mp3

1. Think of 5 appearance words you already know.
2. Listen once without pausing. Identify who is being described and the general topic.
3. Listen again. Write 3–5 appearance words you hear.
4. Say aloud 3 simple descriptions of people you know.

Do not read the transcript before class.
