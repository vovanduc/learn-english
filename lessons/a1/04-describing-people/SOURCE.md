# Source map — A1 Test 4: Describing people

**Level:** A1
**Source:** https://learnenglishteens.britishcouncil.org/skills/listening/a1-listening/describing-people
**Audio:** https://learnenglishteens.britishcouncil.org/sites/teens/files/2024-08/A1_describing_people.mp3

## Source exercise structure
- Preparation: match four people with descriptions of hair/appearance.
- Exercise 1: 4 multiple-choice questions about family relationships and whether people look the same/different.
- Exercise 2: 3 gap-fill items using appearance vocabulary.

## Speaking transfer
Move from identifying people in the recording to describing real people the learner knows, using simple A1 patterns such as **He's got... / She's got... / He's taller than...**.
