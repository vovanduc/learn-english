## Exercises from source workbook

Listen to the conversation and do the exercises to practise and improve your listening skills.

### PREPARATION – Matching

Match the vocabulary with the definition. Write a–c next to the numbers 1–3.

1. ______ band — a. an object like a piano, guitar or drum that you use to play music
2. ______ instrument — b. a time when musicians try to get a part in a band or show
3. ______ audition — c. a group of musicians who play music together

### EXERCISE 1 – True or False

1. There is only one person at the auditions. True / False
2. The band needs a singer. True / False
3. The boy can play the guitar. True / False
4. The boy can play the drums well. True / False
5. The girl says the boy can be in the band. True / False

### EXERCISE 2 – Gap fill

Complete the sentences with words from the recording.

1. 'Ah, band auditions. Great! I'd love to be in a ____________!'
2. 'We need a singer, a guitarist and a ____________.'
3. 'I can play the guitar but I'm not very ____________.'
4. 'Let me hear you play the ____________.'
