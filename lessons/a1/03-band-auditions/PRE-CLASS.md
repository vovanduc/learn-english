# PRE-CLASS — A1-03

**Time:** 5–10 minutes
**Topic:** Music, instruments and abilities
**Primary audio:** https://learnenglishteens.britishcouncil.org/sites/teens/files/2024-08/A1_band_audition.mp3

1. Think of 3 words connected with music.
2. Listen once without pausing. Identify who is speaking and why.
3. Listen a second time. Write 3–5 words you recognise.
4. Say aloud 4 sentences about yourself using: **I can ... / I can't ... / I can try.**

Do not read the transcript before the Voice session.
