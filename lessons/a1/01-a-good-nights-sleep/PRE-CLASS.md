# PRE-CLASS — A1-01

**Time:** 5–10 minutes
**Topic:** A good night's sleep
**Primary audio:** https://learnenglishteens.britishcouncil.org/sites/teens/files/2024-08/A1_A_good_nights_sleep.mp3

## Rules
- Do not open `TRANSCRIPT.md` or `ANSWER-KEY.md` before class.
- Do not try to memorise the transcript.

## Step 1 — Preview (1 minute)
Think about the topic: **sleep / bedtime**. Say 2–3 words you expect to hear.

## Step 2 — First listen (2 minutes)
Listen once without pausing. Answer in your head:
- Who is speaking?
- What is the topic?
- What is the main purpose of the conversation?

## Step 3 — Second listen (3 minutes)
Listen again. Write down 3–5 words or phrases you recognise.

## Step 4 — Speak before class (2 minutes)
Answer out loud:
- What time do you usually go to bed?
- Do you use your phone before bed?
- What helps you sleep well?

Do not worry about grammar accuracy. The goal is to arrive at class with the topic already activated.
