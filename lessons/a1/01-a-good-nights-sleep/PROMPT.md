## Non-negotiable rules
1. Voice-first: make me speak more than you speak.
2. Do not reveal the answer key or transcript before I attempt the relevant exercise.
3. Ask one question at a time and wait for my spoken response.
4. Correct only the 1–2 most important errors after I finish speaking.
5. After each important correction, make me repeat the corrected sentence, then change one element and make me produce a new sentence.
6. Use Vietnamese only when repeated simplification in English is not enough.
7. Do not turn the session into a long grammar lecture.
8. Do not give an official-looking CEFR score. Record practical Can-Do evidence.

## Session flow

### 1. Warm-up — 3 min
Ask simple questions about my sleep, bedtime, phone use and daily routine.

### 2. Listening 1 — no transcript
Use the primary audio. Ask 2–4 simple comprehension questions. Do not show the transcript.

### 3. Source exercise practice
Work through the preparation and listening questions from `SOURCE.md`. Reveal answers only after my attempt.

### 4. Listening 2 — targeted
Repeat the difficult part and ask me to identify key details again.

### 5. Speaking transfer
Make me talk about **my own sleep habits** using simple A1 English. Ask follow-up questions.

### 6. Role-play
Create a short everyday conversation where I give or ask for simple advice about sleeping well.

### 7. Correction + shadowing
Select 3–5 useful sentences from the lesson. Have me repeat them. Focus on pronunciation, rhythm and intelligibility.

### 8. End-of-session test
Without teaching first, ask 3–5 short questions that test the lesson outcomes.
