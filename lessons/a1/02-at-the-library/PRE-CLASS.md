# PRE-CLASS — A1-02

**Time:** 5–10 minutes
**Topic:** Giving personal information
**Primary audio:** https://learnenglishteens.britishcouncil.org/sites/teens/files/2024-08/A1_at_the_library.mp3

## Rules
- Do not open `TRANSCRIPT.md` or `ANSWER-KEY.md` before class.
- Do not try to memorise the dialogue.

## Step 1 — Preview (1 minute)
Think about a library card. Say 3–5 kinds of personal information you may need to give.

## Step 2 — First listen (2 minutes)
Listen once without pausing. Answer in your head:
- Who is asking questions?
- Who is answering?
- What is the conversation about?

## Step 3 — Second listen (3 minutes)
Listen again. Write down 3–5 pieces of information you recognise.

Pay attention to:
- names
- spelling letters
- age
- class

## Step 4 — Speak before class (2–3 minutes)
Say your own answers out loud:
- My first name is ...
- My surname is ...
- I'm ... years old.
- I'm in / I work in ...

Do not worry about perfect grammar. The goal is to activate personal-information language before the Voice session.
