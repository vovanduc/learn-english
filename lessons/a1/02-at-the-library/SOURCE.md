### PREPARATION

Circle the information you think the school library needs to give someone a library card:

First name | Surname | Favourite song | Pet | Class | Favourite book | Age

### EXERCISE 1 – Check your understanding: Multiple choice

1. What is Lucy's surname?
   - a) Morris
   - b) Moore
   - c) More

2. What class is Lucy in?
   - a) 1A
   - b) 2B
   - c) 1B

3. How old is Lucy?
   - a) 12
   - b) 14
   - c) 13

4. What does Lucy need for her library card?
   - a) a photo
   - b) a form
   - c) a letter

### EXERCISE 2 – Gap fill
